Suggested session structure

Check in at both ends.

This agenda gives everyone time to speak and work. The facilitator can adjust the intervals to the group’s size.

11:00–11:15

Opening updates

What did you work on previously? What did you find or conclude? What is your goal for today?

11:15–12:20

Focus block one

Independent analysis, writing, reading, or revision.

12:20–12:30

Short break

Step away and reset.

12:30–1:45

Focus block two

Continue your task or address a specific obstacle.

1:45–2:00

Closing updates

What did you finish? What can you now conclude? What is your next step?

Between sessions

Keep a short record.

Use the same check-in form for an opening goal, a closing update, or an asynchronous progress note.

  • Previous work and output
  • Findings or conclusions
  • Current goal and progress
  • Blockers or feedback requested
  • Next step
